# Legal templates

Starting points for the documents a SecAI instance open to other people should publish. **They are not legal advice.** Laws on security testing, data protection and liability differ between countries: have them reviewed for your situation before you rely on them.

- `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md`: terms for users of your instance.
- `ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md`: what may and may not be scanned.

Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`, then publish them on your site and link them from your sign-up page.
