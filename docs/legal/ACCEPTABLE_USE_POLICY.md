# Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy explains what you may do with the SecAI instance at [INSTANCE URL].

## You may

- Scan websites you own.
- Scan websites you have **written** permission to test, within the scope and time of that permission.
- Share reports with the people responsible for the websites you scanned.

## You may not

- Scan any website without the owner's permission, including to "check" a site you use.
- Try to get around ownership verification, rate limits or the blocklist, for example by pointing a verified domain at someone else's server.
- Use the Service to attack, overload or disrupt any system, or to collect information for an attack.
- Scan websites of critical infrastructure, healthcare, government or financial institutions unless you are responsible for them and permitted to test them.
- Share your account, or use the Service in breach of any law.
- Probe or attack the Service itself outside a coordinated disclosure (report issues to [SECURITY CONTACT]).

## Enforcement

We review abuse reports sent to [ABUSE EMAIL]. We may block domains, pause scanning, suspend accounts and share records of scans (including your recorded authorization, time and IP address) with the affected party or authorities where the law requires.

## Reporting abuse

If this Service scanned your website without permission, email [ABUSE EMAIL] with the domain and the time of the traffic. We'll block the domain and investigate.
